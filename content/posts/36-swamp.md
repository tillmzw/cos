## The Curse

Multiple members of the party are cursed: {{% mdref reese %}}, {{% mdref yaroslav %}} and {{% mdref thaddeus %}}. The party argues among itself for a  while and decides in the end that the amber crypt can wait. They turn around and head for van Richtens tower. They have a quick chat with Buster and meet up with {{% mdref rudolf-van-richten "van Richten" %}} and {{% mdref Ezmeralda %}}. They ask them for help with the curse.

## Salvation in the swamp?

They learn that the curse will turn them into werewolves during the next full moon and their best chance of lifting the curse is a specific plant that grows in a specific swamp. But they should be aware of **Baba Lysaga**, a witch who lives in this bog.

They enter the swamp and try to navigate it among trees and ruins. At first they succeed, but they get lost after a while. While trying to find a way through the marshy terrain, they are suprised by a group of dangerous swamp creatures. The fight against them is hard and the party barely manages to kill them.
