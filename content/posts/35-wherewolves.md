Within seconds, Kiril falls after {{% mdref reese %}}  invades his thoughts before Kiril can call for an attack. Most of the other werewolves flee, and Kiril’s female lieutenant, Zulenka, is slain. Kiril’s younger accomplice is captured and questioned. The party learns that Marc grew up beyond the mists, in the familiar town of Daggerford. He was abducted too, and forced into the ranks of the werewolves. He knows little about the interactions between vampires and werewolves, only that the werewolves exchanged some service to Strahd for his ignorance toward them. After Ilizeiros puts him to death, the other party members notice an ominous curse spreading from the wounds received while fighting the werewolves earlier.

Travelling further into the mountains, Reese notices that his summoned aberration does not disappear. To confirm this observation, he decides to summon another large frog-like being. Neither of those two, nor the humanoid spectre following Ilizeiros around, disappear the way they appeared; however, the humanoid frog-like aberrations eventually decide to leave the camp and search for a solution to their _abduction_ into Barovia.

The party deciphers the first chapter of **The Curse** section of Strahd’s book, which discusses the all-encompassing fog trapping beings and reincarnation.

All those touched by the werewolves’ curse suffer terrible nightmares.
