The voice calls to {{% mdref ilizeiros %}} and supports him interrogating Lady Wachter. She reveals that she made a deal with the vampire bride Anastrasya.

Shortly thereafter Lady Wachter is executed for her crimes. Many of her minions are branded so their crimes are visible for everyone forever. The baron takes over Vallaki as burgomaster once more. This time it will work out. For sure.

The party debates for a long time where to go and decides to go to the amber crypt. Ilizeiros has withdrawal symptoms from the delicious delicious cake that are getting worse.

On the way to the crypt, the party notices that they are being followed. Werewolves! At the edge of the woods the party is surrounded by the werewolf pack that they encountered in the woods around Krezk. Their leader Kiril is with them. He tells them they own him a  life for the werewolves they killed. The party tries to intimidate them but it only works on their lieutenants. A fight ensues...
